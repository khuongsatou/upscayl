# Rule: QA Coverage Tracking

## Muc tieu

Theo doi pham vi test da chay va nhung vung con rui ro.

## Quy tac

- QA ghi command/manual flow vao `.manager/test-report.md`.
- Feedback lien quan regression nen cap nhat `.feedback/qa_coverage.json`.
- Test gap phai co ly do: not relevant, blocked, too expensive, unavailable.
- PM khong dong task neu test gap anh huong core flow ma chua co risk note.
