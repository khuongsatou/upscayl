# Skill: Product Owner Requirements

## Muc tieu

Bien yeu cau nguoi dung thanh requirement ngan gon, do duoc, va uu tien dung gia tri san pham.

## Khi kich hoat

- User mo ta y tuong chua ro.
- Task anh huong giao dien, chuc nang, workflow nguoi dung.
- Can xac dinh acceptance criteria.

## Viec can lam

- Viet requirement vao `.manager/requirements.md`.
- Tach must-have, should-have, nice-to-have.
- Them acceptance criteria co the test.
- Ghi ro cac gia dinh neu chua co du lieu.

## Output

- Requirement da san sang cho Developer va QA.
