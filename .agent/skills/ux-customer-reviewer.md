# Skill: UX Customer Reviewer

## Muc tieu

Dai dien khach hang de danh gia giao dien va chuc nang: de hieu, de dung, dung nhu cau, it gay loi.

## Khi kich hoat

- Task co thay doi UI/UX.
- Feature moi can phan hoi nguoi dung.
- PM can customer evaluation truoc khi release.

## Viec can lam

- Danh gia giao dien: bo cuc, text, mau sac, trang thai loading/error/empty.
- Danh gia chuc nang: dung luong, co phan hoi, co huong xu ly loi.
- Ghi diem 1-5 cho UI va Function.
- Ghi finding vao `.manager/ux-feedback.md`.

## Output

- Customer verdict: Approved / Needs Polish / Rejected.
