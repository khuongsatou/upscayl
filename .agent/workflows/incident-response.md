# Workflow: Incident Response

## Muc tieu

Xu ly loi nghiem trong tren app, release, build hoac pipeline theo cach co log, owner va follow-up.

## Buoc thuc hien

1. PM mo incident trong `.manager/current_task.md` voi severity, impact, owner.
2. QA/DevOps thu thap log va cach tai hien.
3. Developer khoanh vung root cause va dua ra hotfix hoac mitigation.
4. QA verify fix/regression.
5. PM cap nhat `.feedback/responses.md` neu incident den tu feedback.
6. PM viet postmortem ngan trong `.manager/final_report.md`.

## Output

Incident co fix/mitigation, root cause, risk va follow-up.
