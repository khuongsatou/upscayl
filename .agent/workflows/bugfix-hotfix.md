# Workflow: Bugfix Hotfix

## Muc tieu

Sua loi nhanh nhung van co tai hien, nguyen nhan, test regression.

## Buoc thuc hien

1. Ghi symptom, expected, actual vao `.manager/current_task.md`.
2. Tai hien loi hoac ghi ro khong tai hien duoc.
3. Xac dinh root cause va pham vi file.
4. Sua toi thieu, khong refactor lan rong.
5. QA kiem tra case loi va regression gan nhat.
6. PM cap nhat final report.

## Output
